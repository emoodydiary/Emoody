Emoody – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 05/12/25

Emoody (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our mobile application (“App”).

1. Information We Collect
  a. Information You Provide
    We collect information you voluntarily submit within the App, including:
    Mood entries (selected mood, short text diary)
    Photos you choose to upload
    Optional profile details (e.g., display name)
  b. Automatically Collected Information
    When you use Emoody, we may automatically collect:
    Device information (device model, OS version)
    App usage data (screens visited, feature interactions)
    Crash logs to help us improve stability
    We do not collect precise location, contacts, passwords, or sensitive identifiers.
   
2. How We Use Your Information
   We use your information to:
   Provide and improve app features (mood tracking, calendar, statistics)
   Enhance user experience and app performance
   Fix issues and maintain app reliability
   We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing or advertising.

3. Data Storage & Security
   Your mood entries and photos are stored securely on your device or encrypted cloud storage (if syncing is enabled).
   We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access.

4. Third-Party Services
   Emoody may use trusted third-party services to support core functionality, such as:
     Firebase Analytics – for anonymized usage insights
     Firebase Crashlytics – for crash and performance reporting
   These services may collect basic device and usage data to help improve Emoody.

5. Your Rights & Choices
   You have full control over your data. You can:
   View and edit your mood history
   Delete any mood entry or photo at any time
   Request deletion of all your data by contacting us at emoodydiary@gmail.com

6. Children’s Privacy
   Emoody is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Updates to This Privacy Policy
   We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of any changes.

8. Contact Us
   If you have questions, concerns, or data requests related to this Privacy Policy, you can reach us at:
   emoodydiary@gmail.com
   https://emoody.app
