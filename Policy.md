# Emoody – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy explains what Emoody ("the App", "we", "us") does with your information. Emoody is a mood diary. There are no ads and no tracking in the App, and we don't sell your data. The App counts anonymous usage, which you can turn off (sections 3 and 5).

## 1. If you don't sign in

Your pages stay on your phone. That includes your moods, notes, photos, voice notes, carrots, stickers, your reminder choices and what you told us during onboarding. None of it leaves your phone, and deleting the App removes it. The one exception is the anonymous usage counts in section 3, which can include the reasons you picked for using Emoody, but never your pages or moods.

A subscription is needed to write in the diary, and you don't need an account to buy one. See section 3 for what the App Store and RevenueCat handle.

## 2. If you sign in

Signing in is optional. You sign in with Apple, and Apple gives us an account ID, an email address (a private relay address if you choose to hide yours) and your name if you choose to share it.

With a trial or a plan, signing in backs up and syncs these so they appear on your other devices:

- Your diary pages: mood, date and time, notes, prompts, photos and voice notes
- Your carrots, stickers and Bunny's outfits
- Your app profile and settings

They are sent over encrypted connections and stored in your own account. Access rules limit your data to your account. We don't read your diary, and we only look at account data if you ask us to help with a problem or the law requires it.

## 3. Services we use

| Service | What it does | What it receives |
|---|---|---|
| Supabase | Accounts and cloud storage | Your account details and the synced data in section 2 |
| PowerSync | Keeps your devices in sync | The same synced data, passing through to your devices |
| RevenueCat | Manages subscriptions | An anonymous app ID, or your account ID if you're signed in, plus your purchase history, device type and app version. It doesn't receive your diary |
| PostHog (EU) | Anonymous usage counts | Which screens are opened and which features are used, with a random ID made on your phone. It never receives your pages, moods, notes, photos, voice notes or account details. You can turn it off in Me |
| Apple | Sign in with Apple, App Store purchases and notifications | What Apple needs to provide those services, under Apple's own privacy policy |

If you've agreed to share diagnostics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may also share crash reports with us. They don't contain your diary.

## 4. Permissions

Emoody asks for each of these only when you use the feature that needs it:

- **Microphone:** to record voice notes
- **Camera and photos:** to add photos to your pages
- **Saving to Photos:** to save mood cards you choose to save
- **Notifications:** for the reminders you turn on. They are scheduled on your phone

## 5. Your choices

- **Change or delete a page:** you can do this at any time in the App, including after a plan ends.
- **Export your pages:** You can export your pages as a PDF. It's made on your phone and isn't sent to us.
- **Delete your account:** go to Me > Account, or to "Account and deleting my data" when reading your pages without a plan. This deletes your account and everything in the cloud. Pages on your phone stay there until you delete the App.
- **Cancel a subscription:** deleting your account doesn't cancel it. Cancel in your App Store account settings.
- **Anonymous usage counts:** PostHog (EU) receives which screens are opened and which features are used, with a random ID made on your phone. It never receives your pages, moods, notes, photos, voice notes or account details. You can turn it off in Me ("Share anonymous usage"). While it's off nothing is sent, and deleting your account starts a new random ID. Because these counts aren't linked to you, we can't find, copy or delete them for one person.
- **Ask about your data:** you can ask us for a copy of your data, a correction or deletion at the address in section 8.

We keep your cloud data until you delete it or delete your account.

## 6. Children

Emoody is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we'll delete it.

## 7. Not medical advice

Emoody and Bunny offer companionship and reflection, not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. If you're struggling or in danger, please contact a health professional or your local emergency service.
